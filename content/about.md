I'm interested in how work actually happens, including the steps that are missing from the documented process. I want to understand where information comes from, who makes decisions, and what happens when something goes wrong.

## Background

My background combines hands-on development, technical support, and business analysis. I've built user-facing tools, reporting solutions, and software that automates and connects work. I've also helped teams define how they measure success and document how they operate.

That experience shapes how I approach a problem. A recommendation needs to account for what it takes to build a solution, use it day to day, and maintain it when circumstances change.

## How I approach a problem

I want to understand the situation before choosing a tool. Who needs the result? Which decisions require a person? Where does someone have to repeat work or fill in a gap?

I also care about what happens beyond a demonstration. How does someone recover from a failed step? Will another person understand the system when they take it over? Those questions shape the design as much as the intended result.

Sometimes the useful change is software. Sometimes it is a clearer responsibility, a better handoff, or documentation people can use. I look for the simplest approach that meets the need, and I want to know whether it actually helps.

## What I'm exploring

My own projects give me room to test ideas and examine how I work. I want to share the reasoning as well as the results, including what did not work and what led me to change my mind.

I'm interested in my setup, development practices, documentation, and experiments with AI and agents. I currently use AI mainly for brainstorming, planning, documentation, and keeping project context and decisions organized. My use of it in development is more limited. I want to distinguish what works for me from what I can recommend to someone else.

## Working together

I bring the same perspective to helping organizations improve their workflows. I can assess a problem, design an approach, guide implementation, or build the solution where needed.
