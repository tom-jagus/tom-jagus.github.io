## Process & Workflow Improvement

When work gets delayed, responsibilities are unclear, or the same problems keep returning, it helps to understand what is causing the difficulty before deciding what to change.

I look at how the work happens in practice, not only how the process is described. I examine the steps, handoffs, information, and responsibilities, then explain where I see problems and what could improve them. That might lead to a workflow map, revised responsibilities, or a set of recommendations with trade-offs and next steps.

We also decide how to tell whether a change has helped, using suitable measures alongside feedback and observation. The work can focus on one recurring task or a wider workflow. A useful recommendation may involve no new technology at all. I make assumptions and gaps in the evidence explicit.

## System Design & Implementation Guidance

You may know what needs to improve but still need to work out how the solution should operate. I help turn that need into a design people can use to make implementation decisions.

I clarify the requirements and constraints, then describe how the workflow, information, tools, and responsibilities fit together. I work through important scenarios, including what should happen when information is missing or a step fails. Depending on the need, the design might include diagrams, interface descriptions, acceptance criteria, or an implementation sequence.

I can also guide implementation decisions and review how the work matches the design. The detail should help the people doing the work, rather than create documents for their own sake. A new software system is not always necessary, and we can revise the design as we learn more.

## Automation & Integration

Repeatedly copying information between tools or carrying out the same manual steps can be a reason to consider automation. First, I look at whether automating the task is worthwhile and which decisions still need a person.

I design the inputs, outputs, connections, and checks. Where development is part of our agreed scope, I build the script, connector, or focused tool, with instructions for using and maintaining it. A small automation does not have to mean replacing the wider system.

Implementation depends on authorized access and what the connected tools allow. Unattended operation still needs maintenance. We agree on who will own it, how they will notice a failure, and how they can recover.

## Documentation & Operating Practices

Sometimes the instructions exist, but they no longer match how the work happens. Sometimes the process depends on what a few people remember.

I help clarify how the work should happen and write documentation people can use, whether that means one procedure, a runbook, or a team handbook. We also establish who will maintain it. This can be a standalone piece of work; it does not need to accompany a software project.

The people responsible for the work need to check that the guidance is accurate and usable. If the process itself needs substantial redesign, we can address that too. I do not provide legal advice, compliance certification, or accredited training.

## Practical AI Adoption

If you are considering AI for an everyday task, I help assess whether it is useful and how people should review its output. Sometimes conventional automation or a manual approach is a better fit.

My own AI use centers on brainstorming, planning, documentation, and keeping project context organized. I bring that experience to assessing tasks, structuring context and instructions, and defining review steps. Where a trial makes sense, we agree on examples and criteria for judging the results before considering wider use.

If an assistant uses tools or takes actions, we define permitted actions, access, approval points, and stop conditions before a trial. People remain responsible for reviewing its output, and any sensitive information must stay within agreed client and tool policies.

Production integrations and automated actions require a separate assessment of feasibility, capability, permissions, privacy, security, cost, and operational ownership. They are not included by default in this offer.
